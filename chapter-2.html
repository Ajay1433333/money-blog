Meri Khamosh Mohabbat

Chapter 2: Agla Din

Raat bhar neend nahi aayi.

Wo chehra baar-baar aankhon ke saamne aa jaata— wo udaas si aankhen, wo bina muskuraaye bhi mehsoos hone wali maujoodgi.

Subah jab aankh khuli, to pehla khayal ye nahi tha ki office ke liye late ho raha hoon, pehla khayal wo thi.

“Pagal ho gaya hoon kya?” maine khud se hi kaha.

Ek anjaan ladki, jiska naam tak nahi pata, aur main… uske khayalon me uljha hua.

Phir bhi, dil ke kisi kone me ek chhoti si umeed thi— shayad aaj phir usi bus stop par mil jaaye.

Main thoda jaldi nikal gaya.

Bus stop par pahunchte hi, meri nazar seedha us bench par gayi jahaan wo kal baithi thi.

Bench khaali thi.

Dil ke andar kuch sa dheere se toot gaya.

Main khada raha, log aate rahe, jaate rahe, par har aane wali ladki me main uska chehra dhoondhta raha.

Tabhi kisi ne peeche se kaha— “Ye bus late chal rahi hai kya?”

Awaaz… jaani-pehchaani si lagi.

Main dheere se palta.

Wo wahi thi.

Aaj bhi simple suit, baal bandh hue, par aankhon me aaj thodi kam udaasi thi— jaise raat bhar ke bojh ke baad thodi si raahat mili ho.

Mera dimaag khaali ho gaya.

Dil zor se dhadakne laga.

“M-main… haan,” meri awaaz thodi ladkhada gayi, “shayad traffic ki wajah se.”

Usne halki si muskaan di.

Wo pehli baar muskuraayi thi.

Aur us ek muskaan ne meri saari raat ki thakaan chheen li.

“Kal bhi late thi,” usne kaha, “isliye aaj thoda pehle aa gayi.”

Kal. Matlab… usne bhi mujhe dekha tha.

Main kuch kehna chahta tha, par lafz phir saath nahi de rahe the.

“Tum roz yahin se bus lete ho?” usne poochha.

“Haan,” maine kaha, “roz.”

Sach to ye tha ki aaj ye bus stop pehli baar apna sa lag raha tha.

Bus aa gayi.

Is baar hum dono saath chadhe.

Seat alag-alag mili, par dil jaise ek hi jagah baitha tha.

Jaate waqt, usne apna bag sambhalte hue kaha— “Mera naam… Aanya hai.”

Main bas itna hi keh paaya— “Main… kal bataunga.”

Wo thoda hairaan hui, phir muskurayi.

“Kal,” usne dohraaya.

Bus ruk gayi. Wo utar gayi.

Main khidki se use jaate hue dekhta raha.

Us din samajh aaya— kabhi-kabhi kahaniyaan sirf ek nazar se nahi, doosre mauke se shuru hoti hain.

Aur ye kahani… ab shuru ho chuki thi.
