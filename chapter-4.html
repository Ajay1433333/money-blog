📖 Meri Khamosh Mohabbat

Chapter 4: Wo Khamoshi Jo Bolti Hai

Kal bhi.

Uske kehne ka andaaz aisa tha jaise kal milna koi sawaal nahi, ek vaada ho.

Poora din us ek lafz me guzar gaya— kal.

Office me files khuli thi, par dimaag bus stop par hi atka hua tha. Sochta raha— kya wo bhi isi tarah intezaar karti hogi? Ya main hi is kahani ko zyada mehsoos kar raha hoon?

Agli subah main time se pehle pahunch gaya.

Wo nahi aayi thi.

Dil ne kaha— shayad aaj nahi aayegi.

Par phir, thodi door se maine use aate dekha.

Aaj uske chehre par muskaan thi, par aankhon ke neeche thakaan saaf dikh rahi thi.

“Late ho gayi?” maine poochha.

Usne halka sa haan me sir hilaaya.

“Raat thodi lambi thi,” wo boli, aur phir khamosh ho gayi.

Main samajh gaya— ye wo raat thi jiske baare me poochhna abhi sahi nahi tha.

Hum bench par baith gaye.

Shehar jaag raha tha, par hum dono apni-apni khamoshi me doobe hue the.

“Tumhe kabhi lagta hai,” usne achanak kaha, “ki kuch log humse milte hain sirf isliye taaki hume yaad dila sakein ki hum zinda hain?”

Maine uski taraf dekha.

Ye sawaal seedha dil se aaya tha.

“Haan,” maine kaha, “aur kuch log sirf mil jaate hain… bina kisi wajah ke.”

Wo thodi der mujhe dekhti rahi.

“Tumhe darr nahi lagta?” usne poochha, “naye logon se, nayi shuruaat se?”

Maine sach bola. “Lagta hai. Par kuch darr sirf isliye hote hain taaki hum unke aage badh sakein.”

Wo muskuraayi— par is baar muskaan adhoori thi.

Bus aa gayi.

Aaj bhi hum saath baithe, par baatein kam thi.

Bus se utarte waqt usne achanak kaha— “Aarav… agar kabhi main chup ho jaaun na, to samajh lena ki mujhe suna jaa raha hai.”

Main kuch keh paata, us se pehle hi wo bheed me gum ho chuki thi.

Main wahi khada reh gaya.

Uske lafz mere kaanon me ghoonj rahe the.

Us din mujhe ehsaas hua— Aanya ki khamoshi sirf aadat nahi thi.

Wo ek kahani thi, jo abhi bataayi nahi gayi thi.

Aur shayad… wo kahani meri kahani se judne wali thi.
