📖 Meri Khamosh Mohabbat

Chapter 3: Naam Se Pehle Ehsaas

Us din ke baad, bus stop sirf ek jagah nahi raha— wo intezaar ban gaya.

Subah ghar se nikalte waqt dil apni hi raftaar se chal raha tha, jaise kahin pahunchne ki jaldi ho, par manzil ka pata na ho.

Main bench ke paas khada tha jab wo aayi.

Aanya.

Aaj uske chehre par wahi udaasi nahi thi jo pehle din mehsoos hui thi. Aaj wo khud me thodi zyada thi— jaise kisi ne use yaad dila diya ho ki wo akeli nahi hai.

“Good morning,” usne kaha.

Simple sa lafz, par mere liye kaafi tha.

“Morning,” maine kaha, aur pehli baar meri awaaz saath de rahi thi.

Hum dono chup rahe. Par ye khamoshi bojh nahi thi— ye wo khamoshi thi jisme baatein bina kahe ho jaati hain.

“Tum kal apna naam kyun nahi bataya?” usne poochha, aankhon me halki si shararat ke saath.

Main muskuraya. “Shayad isliye… kyunki kuch cheezein jaldi batayi nahi jaati.”

Wo meri taraf dekhti rahi, phir dheere se boli, “Phir aaj?”

“Aaj,” maine kaha, “mera naam Aarav hai.”

Usne naam dohraya— “Aarav.”

Jaise kisi lafz ko mehsoos kar rahi ho.

“Achha naam hai,” usne kaha.

Bus aane me abhi waqt tha, aur waqt… pehli baar kam nahi lag raha tha.

“Tum itni chup-chup si kyun rehti ho?” maine poochh hi liya.

Wo thoda sa ruki.

“Har kisi ke paas kuch yaadein hoti hain,” usne dheere se kaha, “jo wo roz saath leke chalta hai. Sab dikhaane layak nahi hoti.”

Maine kuch nahi poochha. Kuch sawaalon ka jawab sirf waqt deta hai.

Bus aa gayi.

Is baar hum saath baith gaye.

Khidki ke bahar shehar peeche chhoot raha tha, aur mere andar kuch aage badh raha tha.

Bus se utarte waqt, Aanya ne kaha— “Kal bhi miloge?”

Main bina soche bola— “Kal bhi.”

Wo muskurayi.

Aur us muskaan me mujhe pehli baar apni kahani ka agla panna dikhai diya.

Par mujhe kya pata tha— ye muskaan sirf shuruaat thi, aur aage jo aane wala tha wo dono ko badal dene wala tha.
