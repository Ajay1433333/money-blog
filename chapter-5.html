📖 Meri Khamosh Mohabbat

Chapter 5: Adhoori Si Sachchai

Us din ke baad Aanya thodi badli hui si thi.

Wo milti thi, par pehle jaisi baatein nahi karti thi. Muskurati thi, par aankhon tak wo muskaan nahi pahunchti thi.

Main samajh raha tha— ye khamoshi sirf thakaan ki nahi thi.

Ek subah, jab bus stop par hum dono baithe the, usne khud hi kaha—

“Aarav… aaj tum mujhse ek baat pooch sakte ho.”

Mera dil zor se dhadak utha.

“Jo chaaho,” wo boli, “main sach bolungi.”

Sach. Ye lafz sunte hi andar kahin darr sa lagne laga.

“Tum itni udaas kyun rehti ho?” maine dheere se poochha. “Tumhari aankhon me kuch to hai jo tum roz saath leke chalti ho.”

Wo kuch pal chup rahi.

Bus stop par shor tha, par humare beech ek ajeeb si shaanti.

Phir usne gehri saans li.

“Kyuki main pehle kisi aur ki thi,” usne kaha.

Mera seena jaise ek pal ke liye jam sa gaya.

“Mohabbat ki,” wo aage boli, “dil se. Poore yaqeen ke saath.”

Uski awaaz kaanp rahi thi.

“Usne vaade kiye the. Future ke, saath ke, zindagi ke.”

Wo ruk gayi.

“Maine sab maan liya.”

Maine kuch nahi kaha. Sirf suna.

“Par jab waqt aaya,” usne aankhen neeche jhuka li, “to usne kaha— ‘Tum bahut achhi ho, par main ready nahi hoon.’”

Us ek pal me mujhe uski saari khamoshiyan samajh aa gayi.

“Tab se,” usne kaha, “main logon se kam bolti hoon. Kyunki jab bolti hoon, to lagta hai phir se toot jaaungi.”

Maine uski taraf dekha.

Wo ro nahi rahi thi. Shayad aansu bahut pehle khatam ho chuke the.

“Aur tum?” maine poochha, “tum mujhse kyun milti ho?”

Wo thodi der chup rahi.

“Kyuki tum sunte ho,” usne kaha. “Bina sawaal ke. Bina vaadon ke.”

Bus aa gayi.

Is baar hum dono khade rahe. Kisi ne baithne ki koshish nahi ki.

Bus se utarte waqt usne dheere se kaha—

“Main tumse kuch expect nahi karti, Aarav. Bas… agar kabhi door ho jaaun, to samajh lena ye tumhari wajah se nahi tha.”

Wo chali gayi.

Aur main wahi khada reh gaya— is baar sirf uski khamoshi ke saath nahi, balki uske dard ke saath.

Us din samajh aaya— mohabbat sirf paane ka naam nahi hota. Kabhi-kabhi kisi ke tootey hue hisson ke paas khamoshi se baith jaane ka naam bhi mohabbat hota hai.
